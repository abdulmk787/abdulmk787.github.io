01 — About

A quick introduction

I work on reinforcement-learning environments, verifiable agent tasks, and grounded AI products. I am interested in how agents interact with tools and software systems, and how to evaluate whether they achieved the intended outcome.

I moved into Applied AI at Meta in June 2026 after working in Core Ads Growth Engineering. There, I shipped ads growth projects generating $440M+ in annual revenue and developed AI-assisted code migration tooling adopted by 11+ teams. My current work brings that product-engineering experience to environments and data for model post-training.

Alongside environment development, I build across iOS, AWS backend infrastructure, and AI recommendations grounded in structured user data. I earned my MS in Computer Science at USC with a 4.0 GPA, served as a Course Producer for Analysis of Algorithms, and co-authored research on graph learning, cloud simulation, and financial prediction.
